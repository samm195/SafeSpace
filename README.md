SafeSpace is a university web project made by my partner Ben Ahmed ranim and me Abdelli maysam.

We created SafeSpace because we believe personal growth shouldn't feel like a chore. It should feel rewarding!

SafeSpace turns your daily habits, mental health routines, and career goals into an epic quest. You play the game by taking care of yourself!

Inside SafeSpace, you can also freely read articles written by certified therapists, fitness coaches, career advisors, and study experts. Whether you want to beat exam stress, prepare for an interview, or figure out how to get better sleep, our professionals are right here to guide you.
